<p class="mb-8 font-light text-center text-gray-900 lg:mb-16 dark:text-gray-200 sm:text-xl">
    These cities, towns, and counties in Virginia have prioritized their residents' privacy by rejecting the expansion of automated license plate readers.  
</p>

### Staunton

The Staunton, VA City Council [canceled their Flock contract](https://augustafreepress.com/news/staunton-to-cancel-flock-safety-camera-contract-doesnt-agree-with-ideology-of-ceo/) after Flock CEO Garrett Langley sent an [unsolicited email disparaging residents](https://www.ci.staunton.va.us/home/showpublisheddocument/13448) who advocated against the surveillance cameras.

### Charlottesville

The Charlottesville City Council [stopped its Flock pilot program](https://infocville.com/2025/12/19/charlottesville-ends-use-of-flock-system/). Council member [Michael Payne said](https://www.wvtf.org/news/2026-01-15/charlottesville-and-staunton-cancel-license-plate-reading-technology), "It’s a huge problem to have a privately-owned company building a national surveillance system of vehicles and vehicle locations." 

### Warrenton

The Warrenton Town Council voted 5-1 to [reject installing Flock cameras](https://www.fauquiernow.com/news/not-on-our-watch-warrenton-town-council-reverses-course-on-license-plate-readers/article_9a1c02ac-5f77-47c7-a270-06f49ab98332.html). Councilmember David McGuire said, "It’s going to cost us time and money. We’re going to be wedded to this project, and it’s also an invasion of privacy... Nobody is for this."

### Floyd County

The Floyd County Board of Supervisors allowed Floyd County Sheriff's Office [Flock contract to expire](https://www.floydtimes.com/sheriff-craig-flock-cameras-are-out-at-end-of-contract/) after residents brought up privacy concerns. One resident, Jacey Clay, said, "This issue is not whether today's officials will misuse these systems; it's when."

### Elkton

The Elkton Town Council [voted unanimously](https://augustafreepress.com/news/community-group-persuades-elkton-town-council-to-cut-ties-with-flock/) to cancel their Flock contract due to efforts from [DeFlock the Valley](https://deflockthevalley.com/). 

### Harrisonburg

The Harrisonburg City Council announced it supports [Deflock Hburg's](https://deflockhburg.org/) efforts to [remove ALPR cameras from the city](https://www.wmra.org/2026-07-15/harrisonburg-city-council-plans-to-end-use-of-flock-cameras). Mayor Deanna Reed said, "We think that this is what is best for our community. So, all I can say to you is thank you. Thank you for using your voice, thank you for all the letters, the emails, and I think that this is the best decision for our community."

### Wythe County

The Wythe County Sheriff's Office [ended their Flock program](https://www.facebook.com/WytheSheriff/posts/1541769467986082/). Sheriff Anthony Cline said, "After weighing the investigative value of Flock against these constitutional concerns, I believe ending our contract is the right decision for Wythe County."

### Bland County

Bland County Sheriff Jason Ramsey [canceled the department's Flock contract](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/bland-county-sheriff-ends-contract-with-flock-safety/ar-AA2bmm5f/). He said, "I felt the low recovery rate of stolen vehicles and that the ever-changing legal and constitutional questions that might arise in the future, that the use of Flock Cameras was not beneficial for Bland County."

### Charlotte County

The Charlotte County Sheriff's Office [suspended its ALPR system](https://www.wfxrtv.com/news/charlotte-county-temporarily-suspends-flock-safety-cameras/) to provide the public the "opportunity to be fully informed and educated about how the cameras are used." 

### Halifax County

The Halifax County Sheriff's Office [stopped its Flock cameras](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/halifax-county-sheriff-office-pulls-183407809.html) after residents "raised concerns about privacy, government access to information, and constitutional rights." 

### Goochland County

[Goochland County no longer uses Flock license plate readers](https://www.12onyourside.com/2026/08/28/goochland-county-ends-flock-camera-use/). Legal analyst Steve Benjamin noted, "Technology always moves faster than the law. In the meantime, communities are left to listen to their citizens."

### Christiansburg

Christiansburg Town Council voted unanimously [voted unanimously](https://www.wdbj7.com/2026/08/26/christiansburg-town-council-votes-unanimously-remove-flock-license-plate-cameras/) to remove the town’s Flock cameras. A packed public hearing ended in applause. 

### Lynchburg

The city of Lynchburg [suspended its Flock program](https://www.theroanokestar.com/news/lynchburg-police-take-flock-license-plate-cameras-offline/) in order to give time for an independent third-party audit of the department's procedures. 

### King William County

The King William County’s Sheriff’s Office has [removed its Flock surveillance cameras](https://www.dailypress.com/2026/08/24/king-william-county-cancels-contract-for-flock-cameras-after-vandalism-theft/). Sheriff Thomas Lumpkin said, "The cost was too much to continue."

### Giles County

Giles County will [end its Flock contract](https://www.wsls.com/news/local/2026/08/27/giles-county-sheriffs-office-to-end-flock-safety-agreement-on-oct-16/) a year earlier than planned. The sheriff's office said, "Maintaining public trust requires more than determining whether a program can legally be operated." 

### Bedford County

The Bedford County Sheriff’s Office stated it is [suspending Flock camera use](https://www.wfxrtv.com/news/bedford-county-sheriffs-office-pauses-flock-camera-use-cites-data-errors/) due to privacy concerns and discrepancies in vehicle counts.

### Amherst County

The Amherst County Sheriff’s Office is [suspending Flock camera use](https://www.wfxrtv.com/news/amherst-county-sheriffs-office-halts-flock-camera-use/) after an internal audit of the system revealed data errors.

### Bridgewater

The Bridgewater Town Council [unanimously resolved to remove the town's five Flock cameras](https://www.dnronline.com/news/public_safety/bridgewater-town-council-votes-to-remove-flock-cameras/article_239a58f5-3e95-59b7-97ad-2259e3ca41da.html) and not install any similar technology without first engaging in dialogue with citizens. 

### Prince Edward County

The Prince Edward County Sheriff’s Office has [discontinued the use of Flock Safety Cameras](https://www.msn.com/en-us/public-safety-and-emergencies/health-and-safety-alerts/prince-edward-county-ends-use-of-flock-cameras/ar-AA2byck7) in the county. 

### Henry County

Henry County has [suspended the sheriff’s office’s access to the Flock camera system and will not renew a contract](https://www.wdbj7.com/2026/08/14/henry-county-sheriff-suspends-access-flock-camera-system/) with Flock Safety when it expires August 2028. Sheriff Wayne Davis said, "I am not a big-government Sheriff. I am one of the strongest believers in the Constitution of the United States, and I firmly believe in protecting the constitutional rights and individual liberties of every citizen we serve."

### Martinsville

The Martinsville Police Department has decided to [not renew its contract](https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/martinsville-police-won-t-renew-114457383.html) with the Flock Safety when it expires at the end of this year, citing issues of public trust.

### Appomattox County

Appomattox County [canceled its contract](https://wset.com/news/local/flock-fallout-appomattox-and-campbell-pull-the-plug-on-cameras) with Flock Safety. Sheriff Robert Richardson said he did not feel confident he could stand before the community and argue in favor of keeping the cameras.

### Campbell County

Campbell County [canceled its contract](https://wset.com/news/local/flock-fallout-appomattox-and-campbell-pull-the-plug-on-cameras) with Flock Safety. Sheriff Whit Clark said, "Our citizens have made their concerns known, and I believe those concerns deserve to be heard."

### Montgomery County

Montgomery County is [pausing the use of Flock cameras](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/montgomery-county-pauses-use-of-flock-safety-cameras/ar-AA2bwcxR). Sheriff Robert Page said that after hearing residents' concerns about the county's use of the cameras, he is establishing a committee of local officials and community leaders to examine the sheriff's office's current policies and practices surrounding automatic license plate (ALPR) technology.

### Wytheville

The Wytheville Police Department is [suspending Flock camera use](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/wytheville-police-department-suspends-flock-222314887.html) amid ongoing privacy fears. For Police Chief Joel Hash, the decision serves to uphold the "trust and constitutional rights" of the community.

### Dumfries

The Dumfries Town Council voted to [remove Flock license plate reader cameras](https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/dumfries-town-council-votes-4-165438601.html) throughout the community.

### Deflock
Deflock is an organization dedicated to motivating people to remove Flock cameras from their communities. They have a community dedicated to mapping Flock cameras across the United States.   

[Visit Deflock&nbsp;&rarr;](https://deflock.org)

### Database of ALPR Abuse
The Institute for Justice keeps a database with over a hundred instances of ALPR abuse.  

[Visit The IJ Database of ALPR Abuse?&nbsp;&rarr;](https://ij.org/the-ij-database-of-alpr-abuse/)

### When Flock Comes to Your Town
A very thorough article from CNET about what ALPRs are, how they're abused, and what to do about them.

[Visit CNET&nbsp;&rarr;](https://www.cnet.com/home/security/when-flock-comes-to-town-why-cities-are-axing-the-controversial-surveillance-technology/)

### Don't Get Flocked

### Consumer Rights Wiki
The Consumer Rights Wiki has a helpful page with common questions, arguments, and responses when discussing Flock cameras.

### Get the Flock out of Here
Get the Flock out of Here keeps a running list of ALPR abuses and success stories of jurisdictions across the United States that have gotten rid of ALPRs.  

[Visit Get the Flock out of Here&nbsp;&rarr;](https://gettheflockoutofhere.com/)
